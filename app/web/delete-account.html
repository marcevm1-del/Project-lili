Delete your account

حذف الحساب

You can ask us to delete your lili account and your data from here — no need to install the app. You can also do it in the app under Profile → Privacy & Safety → Delete my account.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, name and contact details
  • Your shop and every listing in it
  • Saved items, cart and search history
  • Messages between you and other members
  • Photos you uploaded

What we have to keep, and why

  • Completed orders and invoices — tax law requires us to retain transaction records for several years
  • Consent and agreement records — these are the proof we had your permission, so deleting them would defeat their purpose
  • Moderation decisions — kept without your name attached

Everything kept is separated from your identity wherever the law allows.

Your email app should have opened

The request is written out and addressed to us — it reaches us when you press send. If nothing opened, email privacy@loveitorleaveit.ae with the email address on your account.

We'll reply to confirm it's really you, then delete your account within 30 days. If you still have the app installed, Profile → Privacy & Safety → Delete my account does it immediately, without waiting for us.

Questions: privacy@loveitorleaveit.ae
