# Play listing copy

The public text of the app. `npm run research` runs the `NEVER_CLAIM` patterns
over this file exactly as it runs them over the source, because a sentence on
the store page is read by more people than any screen — and until v2.11.3 this
was the one piece of user-facing text nothing checked.

Nothing here may promise something the app does not do. If a line below stops
being true, the check fails before the listing goes out.

---

## Short description (80 characters)

> Buy and sell pre-loved fashion in Dubai. You meet, you agree, you deal.

## Full description

> **Second hand. First class.**
>
> love it or leave it is where Dubai buys and sells pre-loved fashion. List a
> piece from your wardrobe in about a minute, browse what other women across
> the city are selling, and message a seller or make her an offer.
>
> • Designer resale, everyday pieces, and abayas — one seller, one piece at a
>   time
> • Every listing is screened before it appears
> • You are always told whether you are buying from a private seller or a
>   business, because it changes your rights
> • Message the seller, or make an offer she can accept or decline
> • Bilingual — English and العربية
> • Built for Dubai, priced in AED
>
> **What lili does not do.** lili does not take your payment, hold your money,
> authenticate items, or deliver anything. You and the seller agree how to pay
> and where to meet. We say so on every listing, and we would rather tell you
> now than let you find out later.

## Notes for the reviewer

- lili is an intermediary. It never takes possession of goods or funds, which is
  why there is no checkout and no Play Billing — physical goods sit outside
  Play's billing requirement.
- Listing and messaging require an account. If the beta gate is on at the time
  of submission, the review notes must carry working access.
- Reporting, blocking, appeal and account deletion are all reachable in-app, and
  deletion is also available without reinstalling at the public page in `web/`.
