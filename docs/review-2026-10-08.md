# Database review — 8 October 2026

A read of the code as it runs on the live project, not as the migrations
describe it: all 54 lili functions, every trigger, RLS policy, constraint and
column grant. Fixes are in `supabase/migrations/20261008*_lili_review_*.sql`
(applied) and `supabase/pending/20261008_needs_owner_approval.sql` (not yet).

## Found and fixed (live)

| # | Problem | Effect before the fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rate-limit windows were anchored to the hour | "40 listings / 60 offers / 20 reports per 24 h" was really per **hour** |
| 2 | Screening re-ran on every edit and set status from scratch | Editing the title of a listing moderation **removed** republished it; a sold piece went back on sale |
| 3 | Clients could write `authenticated`, `saves`, `shop_id` on listings | `authenticated = true` switched off the counterfeit price check; fake save counts; a listing could move into someone else's shop |
| 4 | Clients could set message `created_at`, offer `expires_at`/`shop_id`, conversation `shop_id` | Backdated "evidence"; offers that never expire; a conversation pinned to any shop |
| 5 | Direct reports could claim `source = 'auto_screen'` and their own SLA | Skipped the duplicate check and rate limit; jumped the queue |
| 6 | Moderating a conversation report struck the conversation's shop | When a seller reported a buyer, **the seller** took the strike, and was told her shop was suspended |
| 7 | A strike set shop status from that strike alone | Removing a listing from a suspended shop **lifted the suspension** |
| 8 | Dismissing a report set the listing live unconditionally | Resurrected sold listings |
| 9 | Listings of suspended/closed shops stayed `live` | Closing a shop did not stop its pieces being found, saved or offered on |
| 10 | An agreed meet could be edited; finished/cancelled meets could go back to "proposed" | "We both agreed" was not true after the fact |
| 11 | Screening hard-removed lawful listings | "ivory" and "wine" (colours), "crocodile-embossed", "prescription sunglasses", "alcohol-free", "not fake" |
| 12 | Genuine exotic leather was auto-removed | Lawful with CITES papers; now goes to a person (`cites.exotic_leather`) |
| 13 | Hard-blocked listings got a 72 h SLA | The 24 h branch checked for a finding name the screener never emits |
| 14 | No way to mark a piece sold or relist it | `lili_mark_sold(item, sold)` added; pending offers are answered |
| 15 | Invite codes from `random()`; some helpers executable by PUBLIC | Codes now from `gen_random_bytes` without modulo bias; trigger/helper EXECUTE revoked |

Verified on the live database in a rolled-back transaction: all 19 checks
passed (listing guards, screening cases, sold/relist, conversation/message
guards, meet rules, forged report, wrong-person sanction refused, suspended
shop hidden, invite code format, SLA 24/72).

## Needs the owner (one paste into the SQL editor)

`supabase/pending/20261008_needs_owner_approval.sql`

1. **Erasure deleted the other person's conversations.** Deleting a lili-only
   account cascaded through `lili_conversations`, so the woman she spoke to lost
   the whole thread — the opposite of what the erasure receipt says. Changes four
   foreign keys to `ON DELETE SET NULL`.
2. **`lili_withdraw_listing`** — a seller can take a listing down (not while it
   is under review).

The Supabase connector stops for confirmation on `DROP` / `DELETE`, which is why
these were not applied from the session.

## Noted, not changed

- `lili_report_conversation`'s duplicate check matches on shop, so a woman who
  has reported a listing cannot also report a conversation in that shop until
  the first case is decided. Product decision.
- `lili_search` uses `word_similarity(...) > 0.35`, which cannot use the
  trigram index; switch to the `<%` operator once there are thousands of listings.
- Notification copy assumes the other party is a woman ("She did not answer…").
  Fine for the current audience; worth a decision before opening wider.
